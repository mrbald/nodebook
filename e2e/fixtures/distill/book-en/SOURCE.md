Seven essays from *The Federalist Papers* (Alexander Hamilton, John Jay, and
James Madison, writing as "Publius"), 1787-1788: Nos. 10, 15, 39, 47, 70, 78,
and 84 — chosen for real, recurring named concepts across chapters (Union,
faction, republic, the Constitution, the several departments of government,
the judiciary, liberty) that a distill run should link across files.

Text: Project Gutenberg EBook #1404, "The Federalist Papers", plain text,
fetched from <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1404/1404-0.txt> and split one
essay per file, each given a level-1 heading. No wording was changed; only
the Gutenberg boilerplate and the essay's own duplicated title line above the
dateline were left as-is (that duplication is in the original file).
